# Improved job search method

## Source by fit

Start with established employers in `search_policy.json`, then consider other credible companies with relevant work. The list is a sourcing preference, not an endorsement. Do not select companies simply because email application is easy. Use official careers sites and ATS feeds; search snippets are leads until verified.

Search DevOps, SRE, cloud/platform engineering, systems engineering and systems development. MLOps/FDE applications need confirmed specialist experience. Location order remains Remote India, Gurgaon, Delhi, Vizag. Google relocation to Bengaluru/Hyderabad is confirmed; document why other employers qualify for the user's similar-company exception. Check country, city, office cadence, timezone and travel constraints.

## Review and rank

Run `scripts/shortlist.py --as-of YYYY-MM-DD`. It merges saved snapshots and the tracker, deduplicates requisition URLs, preserves receipts, and writes a review queue. Ranking favours company preferences, location and skill overlap, and discounts experience gaps and seniority mismatches. It is a review aid, not an ATS score or an interview probability. Experience parsing is approximate; read the JD to distinguish required versus preferred experience.

Record exact requisition, official URL, location, experience, essential skills, published salary and verification date. Sources older than seven days are flagged; reopen the official posting immediately before applying. Failed fetches and missing feed entries do not prove closure. Search snippets do not establish that a role is live. Unknown salary stays unknown; INR 30 LPA is the candidate target, not a verified employer budget.

## Tailor using evidence

Use confirmed job titles and dates. Select relevant existing achievements without inventing metrics, certifications, GCP skills or production ML experience. Four years of employment does not mean four years with every tool. Review `FACT_CHECK.md`, particularly MLOps claims, project duration and MBA completion.

Midea duties are not clearly mapped in the supplied CVs. Earlier chat prose expanding that role is not evidence: confirm personal responsibilities before adding it to applications. Repository artifacts establish that work exists, not sole authorship or measured results.

Use a readable, single-column CV. Inspect DOCX/text versions before sending. Archive the exact attachment and email with each submission so later CV edits cannot change its history. Older records cannot reconstruct an attachment whose original bytes were not preserved.

## Submit and record

Prefer the employer's official portal. If tools cannot operate it, prepare the packet and give the direct link; keep status unsubmitted. Email only when the employer explicitly accepts applications at that address. Never guess recruiter addresses or use unrelated support contacts.

Check tracker and receipt files for duplicates before sending. Confirmed answers: Programming.com, DevOps Engineer, 30 days, current INR 20 LPA, expected INR 30 LPA. Include current pay only when requested or specifically authorised. Do not infer optional demographic or consent answers.

Authorization persists within its scope. If automatic review requires exact destination/payload approval, show the completed package, explain the rejection and request that approval. Never bypass a rejection. The approved three-company batch on 18 October does not automatically satisfy that exact-destination requirement for new recipients.

Keep discovered, reviewed, prepared, blocked, email sent, portal submitted, replied, interview and rejected statuses distinct. Gmail SENT proves sending, not delivery or employer acknowledgement. Save actual receipts before marking submitted. After a timeout, check receipts and Sent mail before retrying. Discovery/preparation scripts must not send or overwrite submission history.

## Improve results

Aim for 3–5 strong applications per working day. Spend project time closing recurring gaps: Python coding, Linux/network troubleshooting, SLOs and measured recovery. Track recruiter replies, interviews and rejection reasons. Follow up once after 5–7 business days where appropriate; no background sends are scheduled. Do not send duplicate applications to different addresses at the same company to increase volume.

Use evidence and conversion rates to refine targeting once enough responses exist. Report new links, prepared packets and confirmed submissions separately.
